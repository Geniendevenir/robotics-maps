# Coverage gaps: French schools, student associations and research labs

Reviewed on 13 November 2026: `output/ecosystem_france_schools_student_associations_research_labs.json` after verification, containing 118 organizations (33 Research lab, 38 School, 47 Association) and 11 robots/models. Scope is France, including Paris, with existing pilot rows reused rather than duplicated. No rows added or edited; no Notion access.

This is a source-backed coverage review, not a claim that a listed organization will accept a partnership. High means a consequential omission for national coverage or the lab's robot-learning/peer-learning goals; Medium expands research, teaching or practical access; Low covers narrower or historically documented candidates needing a current-activity check. Each bullet is one gap; a grouped bullet counts once, so these are gap counts rather than proposed row counts. **Counts: High 6 / Medium 20 / Low 6.**

## High

- **H1 — ETIS / Neurocybernetics (Cergy):** missing research organization despite ENSEA and ARES being present; its embodied AI, autonomous control and cognitive/social robots directly match the proposed learning focus, with 2025–2026 activity on the [official team page](https://www.etis-lab.fr/neuro/) and ETIS in the [TIRREX directory](https://tirrex.fr/).
- **H2 — Inria RobotLearn (Grenoble, associated with LJK):** missing learning/perception/control team for social robots; multimodal learning and robot interaction provide a complementary research contact to Flowers, Rainbow and HuCeBot, and are not represented by the NAVER or GIPSA rows ([official research description](https://team.inria.fr/robotlearn/)).
- **H3 — INRAE TSCF / ROMEA (Aubière–Montoldre):** missing agricultural/field-robotics research anchor, leaving the national academic map without this alternative to indoor manipulation; the [official unit site](https://tscf.clermont.hub.inrae.fr/) documents agricultural robots and autonomous-control work, and [PAVIN](https://tirrex.fr/plateforme/pavin/) names INRAE TSCF alongside Institut Pascal, so the existing Institut Pascal row does not cover it.
- **H4 — ISM / Systèmes Bio-Inspirés (Marseille–Aix):** omitted explicitly in the original research notes because its site failed; the now-readable [official SBI page](https://ism.univ-amu.fr/fr/equipes/equipe-sbi) establishes bio-inspired robots, GPS-free navigation, perception, mechanisms and actuators, filling a major biorobotics/mechanics gap rather than another generic AI institute.
- **H5 — NAMeC (Gradignan/Bordeaux):** missing student-run RoboCup Small Size League association despite Rhoban and Eirbot being covered; [Aquitaine Robotics' member profile](https://www.aquitaine-robotics.com/member/namec) identifies students from IUT Bordeaux/ENSEIRB-MATMECA, 2024 participation and preparations for 2026, making it a concrete peer for coordinated mobile robots and combined hardware/software work.
- **H6 — RoboBreizh (ENIB / Lab-STICC / CROSSING / LITIS, Brest):** missing RoboCup@Home research/student collaboration despite the umbrella Lab-STICC row; its [official project page](https://web.enib.fr/~robobreizh/src/en/project_home_en.html) explicitly describes movement learning from demonstrations and ROS integration, an important counterexample to the file's largely Coupe de France view of peers; 2026 participation is unknown and needs reconfirmation ([team affiliation](https://web.enib.fr/~robobreizh/src/en/index_en.html)).

## Exclusions and review limits

The original research report intentionally excluded ISIR, Inria Paris/WILLOW, CEA-List, U2IS, Mines Paris/CAOR, Sorbonne, ENSTA Paris, École polytechnique, CentraleSupélec, Mines Paris, École 42 and 42AI because they already exist in `output/ecosystem_paris_all.json`; their presence was checked locally and they are **not new coverage gaps**. Reuse those rows when assembling the national view. Existing Flowers/Larsen/Rainbow/Chroma rows also cover part of their parent labs; old GdR entries such as Lagadic/e-Motion must be checked as aliases/successors rather than added blindly. JRL (Japan) and LAFMIA (Mexico) fall outside this France slice.

Canonical references opened: all ten paginated [GdR laboratory-directory pages](https://www.gdr-robotique.org/laboratoires/?page=1) (49 entries, containing historical names), the [GdR training pointer](https://www.gdr-robotique.org/formations/) and [GdR/Evolis catalogue](https://maformationrobotique.fr/trouver-une-formation/), the [Coupe de France 2023 team list](https://www.coupederobotique.fr/edition-2023/le-concours/liste-des-equipes-cdr2023-senior/), the [French RoboCup federation list](https://www.robocup.fr/en/equipes), [TIRREX](https://tirrex.fr/) and the individual primary pages linked above. More than 15 distinct source URLs were opened; historical directories are discovery evidence, not proof of current operation. Corporate databases/exhibitor lists were not used as the main benchmark for this academic/student slice.

Contemporary reference landing pages also opened: the [September/October 2026 GdR education mapping](https://robotique-et-education.org/cartographie-des-formations-en-robotique-sur-le-territoire-francais/) and the [France 2030 national ecosystem map](https://c-robotique-france2030.org/fr/cartographie-de-lecosysteme-robotique-national-42). Their embedded inventories were not fully extracted; the education page itself warns that its automated indicators need validation. These are useful next-pass resources, not evidence that every plotted institution was audited here.

Saturation stop: three consecutive final discovery queries — “France laboratoires robotique annuaire GdR TIRREX CNRS”, “France écoles robotique annuaire formations GdR Evolis”, and “France associations robotique étudiants annuaire Eurobot RoboCup” — each yielded fewer than 12 additional in-scope organization leads against the original 118-organization baseline (under 10% incremental growth). The training query repeated the catalogue already audited; the student query added ESIEESPACE, now M20. This is discovery saturation for this reference pass, not a completeness guarantee or a current-activity verification of all candidates.

The verifier's existing warnings about club recency, unsupported default “Hackathon host” tags and unknown contacts remain relevant; no willingness to host, lend hardware or mentor is asserted here. The claim that almost no clubs do robot learning needs a broader comparison, especially H6; this pass does not establish prevalence. Research the High gaps first, verify them, and preserve cross-slice deduplication before any approved downstream sync.
